    How to read this

    Trend first. Each cell's top line is the market regime: ▲ Uptrend when price is above the 200 EMA and the 50 EMA is above the 200 EMA and rising; ▼ Downtrend is the mirror; otherwise ↔ Range. A trend only counts when ADX (trend strength) is 20 or more. A ✓ in the badge means a with-trend setup is available.

    Each cell scores the latest candle with 6 rules: trend vs EMA 50, SMA 20/EMA 50 cross, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands and volume. The small number is bullish rules minus bearish rules.

    Trends agree shows how many of the four timeframes are in the same confirmed trend. When short and long timeframes trend the same way, the trend is more established.

    Fresh signals are specific events (crossovers, band breaks, volume spikes). Each one shows how that same event played out on that coin and timeframe in the available history: how often price moved in the signal's direction 5 candles later. Around 50% means no better than a coin flip.

    Trade setups: Entry (EP) is the latest close. Stop loss (SL) sits just beyond the nearest support (long) or resistance (short), kept 1 to 3 ATR away (ATR = typical candle range). Take profits are at 1.5× and 3× the stop distance (risk:reward 1:1.5 and 1:3). Leverage is the highest level that keeps the liquidation price at least twice as far away as the stop loss (capped at 10×). Size the trade so hitting the stop costs about 1% of your account.

    Why the filter: backtests showed that signals going with the trend did clearly better than unfiltered ones, and signals against the trend did worst. The Accuracy check box shows this measured live on the exchange you're viewing.

    Past results don't predict the future, and these are simple rules, not advice. Practise any idea in Paper Trade first.
